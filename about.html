About

Story • Craft • Purpose

I’m a visual storyteller specializing in video and photography for churches, ministries, and mission-driven organizations. My work focuses on authenticity, emotion, and clarity—helping people connect to stories that matter.

I have experience creating documentaries, testimonies, promotional films, and still imagery for both print and digital platforms.

Tools include Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, and professional cinema and photography equipment.
